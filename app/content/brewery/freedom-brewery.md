Based in rural Staffordshire Freedom Brewery are proud to brew real beer, that tastes like beer, with natural ingredients at the heart of our product.  Local spring water, low temperature fermentation, natural carbonation and an extended maturation period of at least 28 days result in our beers offering an unmatched depth of flavour and sessionability.  Our choice of process and the use of time allow our beers to be free from animal products and certified as vegan, representing choice without compromising quality. Independent. Natural. Sustainable.
