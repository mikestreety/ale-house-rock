Founded in 2015 in Bristol’s St Philip’s district, Good Chemistry Brewing has spent the last decade making beer that’s about community, craft and creativity. From lively taproom pours to cans on shelves, we’ve always believed beer should bring people together.  Now heading into a new chapter with experienced hospitality partners behind us, we’re keeping the heart of what made Good Chemistry special - quality, community, character - while moving boldly into the next era. New ideas, fresh energy, same commitment to great beer.  We brew with care, serve with pride and celebrate the local spirit that makes us more than a brewery - a place people call home.
