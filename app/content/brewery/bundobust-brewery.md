Bundobust was born out of a collaboration between two Bradford boys, Mayur Patel and Marko Husak – the chef with generations of Indian food heritage, and the pioneering craft beer bar owner.  Combining their expertise through a series of pop-up events, they spotted a demand for a contemporary update to the classic “beer and a curry”: authentic dishes, boundary-pushing beers, flavours/techniques overlooked in mass-market Indian food/brewing, all served in an informal bar setting. The first Bundobust opened in Leeds in 2014, with five more opening since.   Critically acclaimed and widely regarded as a game-changing enterprise within the independent food and drink industry, Bundobust is a beacon of successful casual dining in the North.
